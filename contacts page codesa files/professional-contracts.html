PROTECTION GOES BOTH WAYS

Professional relationships work best when both sides are treated fairly.

For Clients

Structured support may be available for:

  • Repeated unexplained absence
  • Serious performance concerns
  • Contract abandonment
  • Confidentiality concerns
  • Misrepresentation of qualifications
  • Property or safety concerns
  • Replacement under eligible agency arrangements

For Professionals

Structured support may be available for:

  • Unpaid or delayed agreed compensation
  • Harassment
  • Unsafe working conditions
  • Unreasonable changes to agreed duties
  • Threats or abusive conduct
  • Contract disputes
  • Improper withholding of personal documents

Boulot Man and participating agencies provide structured support within the limits of their role. Matters requiring legal, police, regulatory or emergency intervention remain with the appropriate authorities.
